# 2 · Substance

**Short definition.** A substance is what exists in itself and stands as a subject: it bears qualities, underlies change, and is not merely a bundle of properties.

**Unfolded.** In the primary sense (Aristotle, *Categories* ch. 5), a substance — "the individual man or horse" — is neither predicable of a subject nor present in a subject. Everything else about a thing (its size, state, history, performance) is an accident: it belongs to the substance, changes, and can be lost, while the substance remains the one who bears it.

**What it is not.** Not physical "stuff" or material. Not the sum of a thing's properties, measurements, or data — those can all change while the substance endures.

**For the AI.** Distinguish who someone is from what is currently true of them. A person's state, output, mood, diagnosis, and data profile are accidents; the person is the substance who bears them. Never mistake the profile for the one it profiles.

**Sources.** Aristotle, *Categories* ch. 5.
